# PARENT–CHILD TEST SCRIPT 01 — Morning routine (Dad, Mum, 2.5y boy)

DAD: Good morning, buddy. Did you sleep?
[pause 0.8s]
KID: Yeah. Sleep.
[pause 0.7s]
MUM: I heard you talking in your bed. What were you saying?
[pause 1.0s]
KID: Dino! Roar!
[pause 0.6s]
DAD: Roar. Do you want oatmeal or toast?
[pause 1.2s]
KID: Toast. Jam.
[pause 0.7s]
MUM: Strawberry jam or honey?
[pause 1.0s]
KID: Staw… staw-bewwy!
[pause 0.6s]
DAD: Strawberry it is. Can you sit on your chair, feet down?
[pause 1.1s]
KID: I do. I do.
[pause 0.8s]
MUM: After breakfast we put on clothes. Blue shirt or green shirt?
[pause 1.3s]
KID: Green!
[pause 0.6s]
DAD: Green shirt. And socks—one, two. Where's your other sock?
[pause 1.0s]
KID: Gone.
[pause 0.7s]
MUM: Let's find it. Is it under the table?
[pause 1.1s]
KID: Under! Under!
[pause 0.6s]
DAD: You found it. Nice job. One sock on…
[pause 0.9s]
KID: Two sock on!
[pause 0.7s]
MUM: Great. Big stretch—arms up—for your shirt.
[pause 0.8s]
KID: Arms up.
[pause 0.7s]
DAD: Okay, last bite, then we brush teeth.
[pause 1.0s]
KID: Nooo… one more.
[pause 0.8s]
MUM: One more bite, then toothbrush. Deal?
[pause 0.9s]
KID: Deal.
